Dungeon rules.

The ball carries the item of the last flipper to touch it. A sword hits harder. Fire breaks armor. A purse pays double. A ward saves one drain.

Shoot the left rail to enter the inn. Inside are three items to buy with gold. Hit one, then press the flipper you want to equip.

In dungeons, choose one of three lit loot targets or shoot the exit to leave without loot.
